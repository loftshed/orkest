[orkest-swarm-command]
If the `/swarm` turn hook already opened or closed the Orkest Swarm View pane, confirm the result in one short line and do not call any tools. Otherwise run `ork swarm` (`ork swarm open` or `ork swarm close`) and report the result in one short line.
