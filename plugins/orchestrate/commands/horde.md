---
description: Toggle the Orkest Swarm View split pane or launch a swarm for a goal (/horde [toggle|open|close|<goal>])
argument-hint: "[toggle|open|close|<goal>]"
---

[orkest-swarm-command: $ARGUMENTS]
If the `/horde` turn hook already opened or closed the Orkest Swarm View pane (or if no goal was given), confirm the result in one short line and do not call any tools. If a goal was given, coordinate the swarm for `$ARGUMENTS`.
